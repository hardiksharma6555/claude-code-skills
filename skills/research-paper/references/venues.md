## What each venue rewards

**ICML.** Wants a machine-learning contribution: a learning principle, a guarantee, or an analysis that holds beyond one application. A deepfake paper fits when deepfakes are the testbed for a general ML question. A pure "new detector, higher accuracy" paper is a weak fit. Theory or a clean, explained mechanism helps a lot.

**IJCAI.** Broad AI venue, accepts applied methods with a solid, well-motivated contribution and strong experiments. A new detector with a clear insight and thorough cross-generator evaluation fits well. 7-page main text limit is typical; check the current CFP.

**CVPR/ICCV/ECCV.** Vision method papers; heavier on visuals and benchmarks.

**NeurIPS/ICLR.** Between the two: ML insight valued, applied work accepted if the insight is general.
